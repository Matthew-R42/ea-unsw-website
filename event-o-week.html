What it'll be like

  1. 1

    Grab some food

    It's free. You don't have to sign up to eat it.

  2. 2

    Cast your vote

    Drop a token in the cup for the problem you'd fix first: global health, animals, AI or something else. We count them every evening.

  3. 3

    Find out how rich you are

    Guess where you sit on the world's income ladder, then check. Most people guess low. Try it now.

  4. 4

    Stay for a chat

    Tell us what you're studying and what you care about. Good chats might end with a free book.

Good to know

  • Sign up at the stall: 100% of the money we get from sign-ups goes to effective charities.
  • Drop by for a minute or stay for half an hour.
  • No EA knowledge needed. Most people haven't heard of it.
