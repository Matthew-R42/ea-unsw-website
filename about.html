The society

Students trying to do good, properly.

EA UNSW is a student society for people who want to think seriously about doing good, through their studies, careers and giving. Membership is free.

The story so far

Back, and bigger

  1. 2019

    Founded

    EA UNSW begins at UNSW.

  2. 2026

    Relaunched

    A new team brings the society back, affiliates with Arc, and runs discussion groups and socials through term.

  3. 2027

    The big year

Our sessions

What to expect

Our sessions are built so that a first-time visitor can take part straight away. You will not be the only new person in the room.

  1. Start together

    A short overview or reading on the topic.

  2. Discuss

    Work through the question in conversation.

  3. Decide

    Finish with something to decide, try or follow up on.

House rules

How we do things

Curious first

Questions beat hot takes. "I don't know" is a respectable answer here.

Show your working

Claims come with reasons. If we can't back something up, we say so.

Disagree well

Go hard on the idea and easy on the person. Changing your mind counts as a win.

Bring a friend

Any degree, any year, any level of scepticism. Come as you are.
