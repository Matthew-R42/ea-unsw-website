So what is Effective Altruism?

Effective

Doing what works, and doing it well.

Good intentions aren't enough on their own. We use evidence and careful reasoning to find the ways of helping that achieve the most.

Tap the brain

Altruism

Caring about others, whoever and wherever they are.

Wanting other lives to go well, not because of what we get back, but because they matter as much as ours do.

Tap the heart

Put together, Effective Altruism is using your head and your heart at the same time: caring about others, and caring enough to find out what really helps.
