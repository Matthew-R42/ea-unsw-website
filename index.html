So, what is Effective Altruism?

Tap the brain

Effective

Doing what actually works, and doing it as well as we can.

Tap the heart

Altruism

Caring about others, whoever and wherever they are.

Put together, Effective Altruism is about using your head and your heart at the same time: caring about others, and caring enough to find out what really helps.
