The idea

What is effective altruism?

Effective altruism is a research field and a practical community. It aims to find the best ways to help others, and to put them into practice.

It starts from a simple question: with limited time and money, how can we do the most good? The answers draw on evidence and careful reasoning, and they stay open to challenge.

The society

What is EA UNSW?

EA UNSW is a student society for people who want to think seriously about doing good, through their studies, careers and giving. We began in 2019 and relaunched in 2026.

We run discussion groups and social events during term. We are planning an Intro Fellowship for Term 1 2027. Membership is free.

Where the ideas lead

Causes we discuss

Effective altruism is a way of asking the question, not a fixed list of answers. These are three areas people in the community often work on.

01

Global health and development

People use evidence and careful comparisons to explore ways of improving health and reducing poverty.

02

Animal welfare

People consider how to reduce animal suffering and improve the treatment of animals.

03

AI and other global catastrophic risks

People study ways to reduce risks from powerful technologies and other threats that could affect many people.

Our sessions

What to expect

Our sessions are built so that a first-time visitor can take part straight away.

  • Start together. A short overview or reading on the topic.
  • Discuss. Work through the question in conversation.
  • Decide. Finish with something to decide, try or follow up on.
