Two questions people always ask.

The idea

What is effective altruism?

A research field and a practical community. It asks one question: with limited time and money, how can we help others the most? Then it tries to act on the answer.

The idea in five minutes →

The society

What is EA UNSW?

A student society for people who want to think seriously about doing good, through their studies, careers and giving. We began in 2019 and relaunched in 2026.

Meet the society →

Five tokens. Where do they go?
