Three questions for any problem

Big, overlooked and solvable. That overlap is where an extra hour or dollar tends to go furthest, so that is where people look first.

Malaria→Wild animal suffering→Heart disease in rich countries→Fixing typos on Wikipedia→World peace→Counting every grain of sand→Another gift to Harvard's endowment→Getting the perfect Super Mario 64 speedrun→

Often called scale, neglectedness, and tractability. See 80,000 Hours' post.

Things people ask us

Do I have to agree with all of it?

No. People in effective altruism disagree all the time, and we like it that way.

Is this just about donating money?

No. Your career, your research and your time count as much as your money.

Do I need to know philosophy or economics?

No. Every session is built so a first-timer can join in straight away.

Isn't effective altruism controversial?

It has critics, and some of them have a point. Week 5 of our fellowship is all about it.
